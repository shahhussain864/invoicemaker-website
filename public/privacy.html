Cloud Sync

What Cloud Sync is

Cloud Sync is an optional feature that stores a copy of your business data on our cloud servers and keeps it up to date across the devices where you are signed in. It exists so that you do not lose your records if your phone or computer is lost, damaged or replaced.

Cloud Sync is off by default. No business data leaves your device until you open Cloud Sync in the Application (on Android: Menu → Cloud Sync), choose to enable it, and sign in. If you never enable it, the Application continues to work entirely offline and stores everything only on your device.

Creating your Cloud Account

To enable Cloud Sync you enter your email address. We send a six-digit one-time code to that address, and you enter the code to sign in. There is no password to create or remember.

Your Cloud Account stores only:

  • Your email address
  • A randomly generated account identifier used to tag your data so it is kept separate from other users' data
  • Sign-in timestamps needed to keep your session valid and secure

We do not collect a name, profile photo, date of birth, or any social media profile as part of your Cloud Account.

What is uploaded when Cloud Sync is enabled

Once you enable Cloud Sync, the following business records are uploaded to our servers and kept synchronised:

  • Customers and suppliers — name, phone number, email address, billing address, shipping address, tax identification number, opening and current balance.
  • Your business profile — business name, phone number, email address, business address, business description and tax identification number.
  • Invoices and other transactions — reference numbers, dates and due dates, the customer or supplier the transaction belongs to, subtotals, discounts, taxes, additional charges, totals, amounts paid or received, amounts due, payment status, descriptions, shipping addresses and transportation details.
  • Transaction line items — item name, quantity, unit, price, description, discount, tax and totals.
  • Your item catalogue — item names, codes, units, categories, descriptions, sale, wholesale and purchase prices, discounts and tax rates.
  • Stock adjustments and item categories.
  • Expenses — expense categories, expense items and amounts.

Please read this carefully. The records above include Customer Data — personal information about your own customers and suppliers, such as their names, phone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses. When you enable Cloud Sync, that information is uploaded to our servers along with the rest of your business records. Please see Your Customers' Data below for what this means for your own legal responsibilities.

Business logo and signature images

If you have added a business logo or a signature image to your business profile, those two image files are uploaded to our cloud file storage so they can appear on your invoices on your other devices.

These files are stored in a private storage area. They are not published, not indexed by search engines, and cannot be opened by anyone who is not signed in to your Cloud Account. Each account's files are kept in a separate folder identified by your account identifier.

What is NOT uploaded

The following stay on your device only and are never sent to our servers:

  • Generated PDF invoices, receipts and reports. These are created on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to share, email, print or save them.
  • Excel and CSV exports. Same as above.
  • Your application settings — invoice print settings, thermal printer settings, home screen layout, report filters and general preferences.
  • Your device's phone book. Only contacts you explicitly select become customers.
  • Camera images used for barcode scanning.
  • Google Drive backup files. These go to your Google Drive, not to us. See below.

When Cloud Sync runs

Once you have enabled it, Cloud Sync runs automatically and continuously while you are using the Application — there is no separate "sync now" button to press. Changes you make are sent as you save them, and changes made on your other devices are received while the Application is open. Syncing pauses while the Application is in the background and resumes when you return to it.

Choosing how your existing data is merged

The first time you enable Cloud Sync on a device, the Application asks you to choose one of two options:

  • Use the data from the cloud — the data currently on this device is replaced with the data from your Cloud Account.
  • Use the data from this device — the data currently in your Cloud Account is erased and replaced with the data from this device.

Please choose carefully: both options overwrite data, and the change cannot be undone by us.

Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.

The measures we apply include:

  • Encryption in transit. All communication between the Application and our servers, Google Drive, Firebase and Paddle uses HTTPS/TLS. The Website is served over HTTPS only.
  • Encryption on your device. The Application's local database is stored in encrypted form, on Android and on Windows.
  • Account isolation. Every synced record is tagged with your account identifier, and our servers enforce access rules so that your records and your uploaded logo and signature images can only be read by your signed-in account.
  • Private file storage. Logo and signature images are held in a private storage area that is not publicly accessible.
  • No passwords to steal. Cloud Sync uses one-time email codes rather than stored passwords.
  • No automatic device backup. The Android Application opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your business database is not copied to Google's backup service without your involvement.

Please note that Cloud Sync is not end-to-end encrypted. Your data is encrypted in transit and protected by access controls at rest, but it is stored in a form that our infrastructure can process in order to provide the sync service.

Backup files stored in Google Drive are under your Google account and are protected by Google's security protocols. We do not store your Google account credentials or access any data beyond what is required for backup functionality.

You are responsible for keeping access to your device and to your email account secure, since anyone with access to your email inbox could request a sign-in code.
